# EverestLabs
> EverestLabs uses AI and robotics to help recyclers ensure high-quality feedstock for metal and plastics recycling processes.

EverestLabs provides AI-powered sorting and tracking solutions for Materials Recovery Facilities (MRFs), recyclers, and packaging manufacturers. Our technology improves the quality and traceability of recycled materials.

- [Materials Recovery Facilities](https://www.everestlabs.ai/materials-recovery-facilities): AI solutions for MRFs

- [Packaging Manufacturers](https://www.everestlabs.ai/packaging-manufacturers): Recycled content verification
